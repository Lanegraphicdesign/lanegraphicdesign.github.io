Spatial typographic experiment
THE MORE STARS

This text-based AR experience is inspired by the motion of washing machines and how it speaks to the relentless gesture of consumption and labour. Situated at the parking spaces on the corner of Grant Street, it brings fragments of the language in domestic life into a utility-based public setting. The experience consists of four parts, all corresponding with each other through the cyclical motion or imagery of water. It begins with the phrase “the more stars, the more energy efficient,” a figurative and ironic slogan addressing the obsession with efficiency, spinning sideways right in front of you. Looking back, “do not swallow,” a warning associated with fluids, spins above the busy intersection as an looming, subliminal presence. In addition, “a cool dry place”, a description of an ultimate ideal condition, follows you like the moon. Finally, a quote from Woolf’s The Waves further down the pavement counters the mechanical repetition with the ceaseless movement of human consciousness. All text respond to the structural and ephemeral qualities of the site, with the roads, traffic, trees and daylight taken into account as a whole experience.
