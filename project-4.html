Graphic Intervention
Southbank News × VCA Special Edition

Drifting through the city and observe is my favourite thing to do other than designing. This initiative offers an opportunity to visually interpret my observations on the neighbourhood on the local newspaper. From my fieldwork, what stands out the most is how the orderly, abstract, and industrial is inevitably countered with the living organisms. The various kinds of road markings around Southbank were extracted into basic forms, bringing elements of the street and traffic onto the paper. The hard, regulatory lines and blocks are contrasted with tacky, cartoonish bees wandering across the spread.
