Melbourne Design Week Program
CLOSENOUGH

Created for Melbourne Design Week 2026, this typographic mural critically reflects on how a foreign language is usually perceived in a multilingual environment. By placing a series of visually similar Chinese characters side by side, the work shifts the audience's traditional way of looking to an active, close observation of formal proximity. The glyphs are made with humble everyday materials, loose threads from my own clothes, that are magnified to a macro scale.
