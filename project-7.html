How To Have Bird Neighbors 2026 Edition

This handmade book is a revival of a bird watching manual originally published around 1900, digitised by the Gutenberg Project. Its positioning is quite the opposite of Fox Trapping 2025 Edition, all criticality is dissolved into the haziness and warmth in the multilayered, low-res photos taken by the author. Every page is hand-woven, inspired by the documentation of bird nesting and the presence of human gestures in the original narrative.
